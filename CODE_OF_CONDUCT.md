# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

DawnDesk should be a respectful, focused place to build. Everyone participating in the project is expected to communicate with care, assume good intent, and keep feedback specific to the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome beginners and experienced contributors alike.
- Give feedback on code, docs, design, and decisions without personal attacks.
- Credit other people's work.
- Keep discussions relevant to DawnDesk and its community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that makes the project unsafe or hostile.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that violates this code, report it to the project maintainer. Reports should include what happened, where it happened, and any useful context.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other action needed to protect the project.
