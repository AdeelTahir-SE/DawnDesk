# Finance Manager App Features
> Features of tools like Oracle NetSuite, SAP, QuickBooks Enterprise, and Sage Intacct — covering the full financial management lifecycle.

---

## 1. General Ledger

The spine of any finance platform — every transaction flows through here.

- Chart of accounts creation and management
- Journal entry creation, editing, and posting
- **Multi-currency** support with exchange rate management
- Period-end close management and checklists
- Inter-company eliminations for consolidated entities
- Full audit trail on every transaction
- Fiscal year and accounting period configuration
- Reversing journal entries
- Statistical accounts for non-monetary data

---

## 2. Accounts Receivable

Track money coming in — from invoice to collection.

- Invoice creation and customization (branding, line items, taxes)
- Recurring billing schedules and subscription invoicing
- Payment terms configuration (net 30, net 60, etc.)
- Credit memo and invoice adjustments
- Aging reports: 30 / 60 / 90 / 120+ day buckets
- Revenue recognition scheduling (ASC 606 / IFRS 15)
- Dunning workflows and automated payment reminders
- Customer statements and account summaries
- Cash application and unapplied payments management

---

## 3. Accounts Payable

Track money going out — from vendor bill to payment.

- Vendor bill entry and GL coding
- **3-way PO matching**: purchase order → goods receipt → vendor invoice
- Scheduled payment runs (weekly, monthly)
- Early payment discount tracking and capture
- Vendor credit management and debit memos
- Payment hold, release, and approval queues
- Vendor aging reports
- Duplicate invoice detection
- Electronic payments: ACH, wire, check printing

---

## 4. Cash & Treasury

Visibility and control over actual cash positions.

- Bank account setup and management
- Bank reconciliation (manual and automated)
- Cash flow forecasting (short-term and long-term)
- Electronic bank feed import and auto-categorization
- Multi-bank account consolidation and sweeping
- Petty cash management and reimbursement
- Wire transfer and ACH tracking
- Interbank transfer recording
- Cash pooling across subsidiaries

---

## 5. Budgeting & Forecasting

Plan finances and measure against actuals.

- Annual budget creation at account and department level
- Budget vs. actual variance reports
- Rolling forecast models (monthly updates)
- Scenario planning: best case, worst case, base case
- Department-level budget allocation and ownership
- Budget revision tracking with version history
- Top-down and bottom-up budgeting approaches
- Driver-based forecasting (headcount, units, etc.)
- Capital expenditure (CapEx) budgeting

---

## 6. Financial Reporting

Generate and distribute standard and custom financial statements.

- Income statement (Profit & Loss)
- Balance sheet
- Cash flow statement (direct and indirect method)
- Trial balance report
- Custom report builder with drag-and-drop fields
- Consolidated multi-entity and multi-subsidiary reports
- Comparative period reporting (month-over-month, year-over-year)
- Scheduled report delivery via email
- Export to PDF, Excel, and CSV
- XBRL / regulatory filing support

---

## 7. Fixed Assets

Track the full lifecycle of physical and intangible assets.

- Asset register / asset book management
- Depreciation schedules: straight-line, declining balance, units of production
- Asset acquisition, capitalization, and disposal
- Asset revaluation and impairment recording
- Asset tagging and physical location tracking
- **Lease management** (IFRS 16 / ASC 842 compliance)
- Bonus depreciation and Section 179 (US)
- Asset transfer between departments or locations
- Gain/loss on asset disposal calculation

---

## 8. Tax Management

Automate tax calculation and simplify compliance.

- Tax code configuration: GST, VAT, sales tax, use tax
- Automated tax calculation on invoices and bills
- Tax reporting summaries and filing prep
- Withholding tax tracking and certificates
- Input / output tax reconciliation
- Multi-jurisdiction and multi-country tax rules
- Tax exemption certificate management
- Nexus tracking for US sales tax compliance
- Integration with tax engines (Avalara, Vertex)

---

## 9. Procurement

Control spending from request to payment.

- Purchase requisition creation and routing
- Purchase order creation, approval, and tracking
- Vendor catalog and price list management
- Goods receipt and inspection recording
- Vendor contract management and expiry tracking
- Spend analytics by category, vendor, and department
- Blanket purchase orders for recurring spend
- Preferred vendor and sourcing rules
- Budget check on PO creation

---

## 10. Inventory & Cost of Goods Sold

Accurately value stock and cost products.

- Inventory valuation methods: FIFO, LIFO, weighted average
- Stock-level tracking and reorder point alerts
- Cost of goods sold (COGS) calculation and posting
- Inventory adjustments and write-offs
- Multi-warehouse and multi-location support
- Landed cost allocation (freight, duties, insurance)
- Kitting and assembly cost tracking
- Slow-moving and obsolete inventory reports
- Physical inventory count and variance reconciliation

---

## 11. Integrations & Automation

Connect the finance stack and eliminate manual work.

- Bank feed auto-import and transaction categorization
- ERP / CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.)
- Payroll system sync (ADP, Gusto, Workday)
- REST API access for custom integrations
- Automated recurring journal entries
- Import / export via CSV, XML, and EDI
- E-commerce platform sync (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon)
- Expense management tool integration (Concur, Expensify)
- Workflow automation: if X then Y rules on transactions

---

## 12. Compliance & Audit

Maintain financial integrity and meet regulatory standards.

- SOX compliance controls and segregation of duties
- GAAP and IFRS reporting standards support
- Full immutable transaction audit trail
- Locked period protection (prevent backdated edits)
- Data retention policies and archiving
- External auditor read-only access provisioning
- Electronic document storage linked to transactions
- Internal control testing and sign-off workflows
- Regulatory filing support (10-K, 10-Q, statutory accounts)

---

*Finance manager platforms like Oracle NetSuite, SAP Business One, Sage Intacct, and QuickBooks Enterprise cover all of the above. Feature depth varies by tier and module purchased.*
